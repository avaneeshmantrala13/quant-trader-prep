# Firm Timed Assessments — Part 2 (Undergrad Quant-Trader OAs)

> **Scope.** Research deliverable to INFORM a timed-drills / "Speed Arena" feature. Covers the **timed online-assessment (OA) formats** and **timed interview elements** in the **undergraduate quant-TRADER intern** pipelines of a defined set of firms (Part 2 — see list). Not for researcher/dev tracks except where a firm's only surfaced undergrad seat is research/dev (flagged).
>
> **Deliberately NOT re-detailed here (Part 1, covered by a parallel worker):** Optiver, Jane Street, Citadel Securities/Citadel, Jump, IMC, SIG, DRW, HRT, Five Rings, Akuna. Their well-known formats are referenced only in the cross-firm synthesis (§B/§C).
>
> **Freshness & confidence.** Research date **2026-07-28**. OA formats rotate; firms change tests without notice. Question counts / time limits are point-in-time, mostly **community-reported** (prep vendors, candidate reports on WSO/Glassdoor). Treat *substance* (skill categories tested, timed vs. untimed) as durable; treat exact counts/timers as illustrative. Confidence is tagged per claim. Nothing here is fabricated; gaps are stated as gaps.
>
> **A note on one recurring source:** `tradinginterview.com` **officially hosts** Flow Traders' and (via its prep course) mirrors Maven/Akuna OA formats — so its format descriptions for those two firms are unusually close to primary. It is still a commercial prep vendor; tagged accordingly.

### Flow Traders — *Graduate / Trader Intern (Quant Trader track)*
**Timed OA: YES (well-documented). Platform: `tradinginterview.com` (Flow's official OA host) + HackerRank for tech roles.**
**Confidence: High** for format (official host + firm careers page corroborate a "numerical test" of mental math + sequences).

- **Structure (two timed numerical sub-tests, taken as Round 1):**
  1. **Mental Math Test** — **60 questions in 6 minutes** (~6 s/question), **one at a time, no skipping / no going back**, multiple-choice, **no calculator** (pen & paper OK). Content: basic arithmetic on **integers, decimals, and fractions**. This is squarely in the Optiver-80/8 family but shorter and non-penalty-framed. `(vendor/official-host, 2026-07)`
  2. **Sequences Test** — pattern-recognition / logical progression, **~26 questions in ~25 minutes** (one vendor page; longer per-question budget than the math test → these are harder multi-step patterns, not speed arithmetic). `(vendor, 2026-07; count/timer soft)`
- **Firm's own guidance** (flowtraders.com/careers): "Complete a numerical test to evaluate your **mental math and sequences** skills"; recommends practicing mental math, playing "games of statistics and strategy," and joining trading competitions. `(official, 2026-07)`
- **Later rounds (timed elements):** Trader interview covers **brainteasers + problem-solving + business knowledge**; program includes **trading simulations**. `(official)`
- **Difficulty:** speed-dominated on the math test (6 s/q with no skip is aggressive); sequences reward pattern fluency over arithmetic.
- **Categories hit:** fast mental math (int/decimal/fraction), **sequences/pattern recognition** (heavy), brainteasers (interview).
- **Sources:** [flowtraders.com/careers/graduates](https://flowtraders.com/careers/graduates/) (official); [tradinginterview.com Flow OA](https://www.tradinginterview.com/flow-traders-online-assessment/) & [Flow math-test tool](https://www.tradinginterview.com/courses/mental-arithmetic/quizzes/flow-traders-math-test/) (vendor/official-host).

- **Sources:** [mavensecurities.com/emerging-talent](https://www.mavensecurities.com/emerging-talent/) (official); [tradinginterview.com Maven OA](https://www.tradinginterview.com/maven-securities-online-assessment/) (vendor); [aptitude-test-prep.com Maven numerical](https://aptitude-test-prep.com/employers/trading-assessments/maven-numerical-test/) (vendor).

### Old Mission Capital — *Micro-Internship / Junior Quant Trader*
**Timed OA: YES (community-reported, two-part). Difficulty reputation: among the hardest.**
**Confidence: Medium-High** (multiple independent candidate reports + a detailed prep guide; exact counts fuzzy).

- **OA structure (community):** two parts — **(1) a timed math section** ("~ a dozen+ questions": typical probability/statistics + math puzzles) and **(2) a coding question** (e.g., "return the running median as you add elements to a list"). `(community: WSO, 2026)`
- **Prep-guide framing:** an **initial timed mental-math + basic-probability test** described as "**similar to Optiver's 80-in-8 in spirit — rapid-fire arithmetic under severe time pressure**"; it's a **hard filter**. `(vendor: Quant Blueprint, 2026-07)`
- **Interview timed elements (well-attested):** **market-making game** — interviewer buys/sells against your bid-ask, you re-quote each iteration while tracking **P&L, max drawdown, long/short exposure, weighted-avg price**; plus **Fermi/estimation** and **confidence-interval elicitation** ("give a 90% CI for average S&P return / a team's win count"). One candidate was rejected specifically for a "too weak" market-making round. `(community: WSO, 2026)`
- **Difficulty:** high; probability is multi-step (conditional prob, Bayes, EV with complex payoffs, optimal stopping), and the timed arithmetic gate is strict.
- **Categories hit:** fast mental math, probability/EV under time, **combinatorics**, **estimation/Fermi**, **confidence-interval / calibration**, **market-making** (interview), light coding.
- **Sources:** [quantblueprint.com Old Mission guide](https://www.quantblueprint.com/guides/how-to-get-a-job-at-old-mission) (vendor); [WSO Old Mission interviews](https://www.wallstreetoasis.com/company/old-mission-capital/interview) (community).

### Belvedere Trading — *Quant Trading / Junior Trader Intern (Options MM)*
**Timed OA: YES (specific numbers reported). Interview is arithmetic-heavy throughout.**
**Confidence: Medium-High** (one detailed prep guide with concrete numbers; corroborated qualitatively by firm profile).

Trader track also gets brainteasers + short market-intuition/market-making discussion. `(vendor: Quantt, 2026-07)`
- **Difficulty:** moderate OA bar (higher pass rate than XTX/Old Mission) but sustained arithmetic pressure across the live day.
- **Categories hit:** fast mental math (2-digit mult emphasis), probability/EV under time, **sequences/pattern recognition**, market-making intuition (interview).
- **Sources:** [quantt.co.uk Belvedere interview](https://www.quantt.co.uk/resources/belvedere-trading-interview) (vendor/community-synthesis).

## Part B — Cross-firm synthesis: recurring TIMED skill categories (top-20 landscape)

Ranked by how frequently/heavily the category appears as a **timed** element across the whole top-20 (Part 1 + Part 2). Part-1 formats are folded in from general public knowledge (Optiver ~6–7 timed modules incl. probability-under-1.5-min, arbitrage-from-spreads, estimation/Fermi, sequences, Zetamac-style mental math, 80-in-8 sprints; Jane Street ~7-min Zetamac gate + "state your confidence"; SIG/IMC/Jane Street market-making games; Akuna/Maven 80-in-8 family; Citadel 50-Q/12-min mixed OA).

| Rank | Timed skill category | How it shows up | Prevalence (firms/heaviness) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Fast mental arithmetic (int/decimal/fraction/%)** — the *gate* | 60-in-6 / 80-in-8 / 50-in-5 speed tests; rapid-fire arithmetic through live days; "2-digit mult < 3 s" | **Near-universal.** Optiver, JS, Flow, Maven, Belvedere, PEAK6, CTC, Wolverine, Old Mission, Akuna, IMC, SIG, Citadel. |
| **2** | **Probability / EV under time** | Timed prob sections (Maven 18Q/30min, Citadel mixed OA), rapid EV/dice/card questions in interviews, Optiver probability-under-1.5-min | **Very high.** Almost every firm, OA and/or interview. |
| **3** | **Market-making game (quote a spread, update on info, manage inventory/P&L)** | Live/mock timed exercise; re-quote each round; track P&L/drawdown/exposure | **High** (options MMs). JS, SIG, IMC, Optiver, Citadel, CTC, Wolverine, Old Mission, Belvedere, Akuna, Five Rings, (Group One inferred). |
| **4** | **Sequences / pattern recognition** | Dedicated timed sequences tests (Flow ~26/25min, Maven 30/6min), sequences inside cognitive batteries (PEAK6), Optiver-family sequences | **High.** Flow, Maven, PEAK6, Optiver, Akuna, (Belvedere pattern-recognition), CTC (aptitude). |
| **5** | **Estimation / Fermi / market-sizing** | "How many…"; CI elicitation; approximations in Maven numerical | **Medium-high.** Optiver, Jump, HRT, Citadel, PEAK6, Old Mission (CIs), Maven (approximations). |
| **6** | **Arbitrage / spread reading / de-vig ("no-arbitrage" from quotes)** | Optiver-style "derive fair value / spot the arbitrage from a set of prices/spreads" timed module | **Medium** (heaviest at Optiver; implicit in every market-making round; explicitly a distinct OA module mainly at Optiver-family). |
| **7** | **Logic / matrix / critical-thinking puzzles** | IQ/cognitive batteries (PEAK6, CTC aptitude), grid/matching logic | **Medium.** PEAK6, CTC, Wolverine, (Citadel critical-thinking). |
| **8** | **Combinatorics / counting under time** | Counting problems inside prob sections; harder at research-leaning firms | **Medium.** Old Mission, XTX, Citadel, and inside most probability sections. |
| **9** | **Rules reading-comprehension under time** | "Read the game rules fast, then act" (embedded in trading-game instructions, some cognitive tests) | **Low-medium / implicit.** Rarely a standalone module; surfaces inside trading games and instruction-heavy OAs. |
| **10** | **Coding / algorithmic under time** | HackerRank/CodeSignal (Wolverine tech, Maven, Flow tech), XTX/Two Sigma/Virtu/Tower research-dev | **Role-dependent.** Gate for research/dev tracks; "a plus" (not the timed gate) for pure trader tracks. |

**Durable takeaways:**
- The **arithmetic speed gate (#1)** and **probability/EV under time (#2)** are the two universal timed filters — *every* market-maker in scope screens on them.
- **Sequences (#4)** are more common than the Speed Arena's arithmetic-only framing implies — Flow, Maven, PEAK6, Optiver-family all test them as a *distinct* timed skill with a *longer* per-question budget (reasoning, not raw speed).
- **Market-making games (#3)** are the highest-signal *interview* timed element and are almost entirely unserved by pure arithmetic drills.
- **Arbitrage/de-vig (#6)** and **estimation/Fermi (#5)** are Optiver-signature timed modules with clear community demand but essentially no good free trainers.

## Part C — Feature recommendations vs. the existing Speed Arena

### C.1 What the Speed Arena already is (grounded in current source)
Read from `src/lib/arena/config.ts`, `src/content/arena/generators.ts`, `src/pages/SpeedArenaPage.tsx`, `src/components/arena/*`, `src/content/arena/firmFormats.ts`, and prior planning in `DESIGN_TIMING_LEADERBOARD.md` + `FIRM_REQUIREMENTS.md`.

**Currently implemented (arithmetic-only speed engine):**
- **Modes:** `zetamac` (fixed-window count, no penalty), `optiver` (80 Q / 8:00, +1/−1, skips free, pass≈56 / competitive≈70), `custom` (choose duration `[30,60,120,300,600]`, ops `+ − × ÷`, packs `int/fraction/decimal/percent`, penalty on/off).
- **Engine quality:** deterministic seeded streams; **fraction/decimal/percent packs extend Zetamac** (which is int-only); free-entry + MCQ; per-op operand ranges.
- **Analytics (strong, already built):** score + pass/competitive markers, accuracy, avg/median/p90 time, per-question time strip, error breakdown by op, pacing (required vs. actual → projected reach), **rushing-mistake detector** (`wrong && rt < 0.5×user_median`, floor 800 ms), **careless-ratio nudge**, **EV coaching** for penalty mode (skip > guess when unsure), 7-day-median trend + local PB.
- **Leaderboard:** server-authoritative ranked runs (integer-only eligible), Zetamac-count board + Optiver-penalty board, weekly leagues + PB, AWS-free-tier design.
- **Firm attribution:** `firmFormats.ts` currently maps ONLY `optiver`→"80/8 sprint" and `zetamac`→"fixed-window arithmetic", both `confidence: low`, clearly flagged community-reported. **No Flow / Maven / Belvedere / others yet.**

**So the Speed Arena today = category #1 (mental arithmetic) done very well, plus the analytics scaffolding (timing, penalty, rushing detection, leaderboard) that any new timed drill can reuse.**

### C.2 Category → feature map (COVERED vs. GAP)

| # | Category | Speed Arena status | Concrete timed-drill feature idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fast mental arithmetic | ✅ **Covered well** (Zetamac + Optiver + custom packs) | **Add firm presets** that already exist in the wild: **Flow 60/6 (no-skip, MCQ)**, **Maven 50/5**, **Belvedere ~35/60min mixed** — these are trivial new `ArenaPreset`s + `firmFormats.ts` entries. Add a **"no-skip / no-back" toggle** (Flow-style) and **MCQ-vs-free-entry** toggle. **Highest ROI, lowest effort.** |
| 2 | Probability / EV under time | ❌ **GAP** (arena is arithmetic-only; reasoning tabs are untimed) | **Timed Probability Sprint**: a soft-timer probability/EV mode (e.g., dice/cards/EV, "answer in 90 s"), reusing the pacing + rushing analytics. Mirrors Maven's 18Q/30min prob test and Optiver's probability-under-1.5-min. Keep leaderboard OFF (not directly comparable) but keep per-question timing. |
| 3 | Market-making game | ❌ **GAP** (biggest interview-signal gap) | **Timed Market-Making drill**: bot posts a fair value + noise, you quote a two-sided spread, it lifts/hits and reveals new info; you **re-quote each round** while the UI tracks **P&L / max drawdown / inventory / weighted-avg price** (exactly the Old Mission/Wolverine interview loop). Score = risk-adjusted P&L under a shot clock. |
| 4 | Sequences / pattern recognition | ❌ **GAP** | **Timed Sequences drill**: numeric **and alphabetic** sequences (Maven), ~30 Q with a *longer* per-item budget than arithmetic. New generator + preset; reuses timer/analytics. Flow/Maven/PEAK6/Optiver all test this. |
| 5 | Estimation / Fermi | ❌ **GAP** | **Timed Estimation** with **interval elicitation** (ties to the planned Calibration Gym): "give a 90% CI in 60 s," scored on calibration + speed. Serves Optiver/Jump/HRT/Old Mission (CIs). |
| 6 | Arbitrage / spread / de-vig | ❌ **GAP** | **Timed Arbitrage drill**: show a small set of quotes/spreads (or odds) and ask "fair value / where's the arb / de-vig these odds" under ~30–60 s. Optiver-signature; no good free trainer exists. |
| 7 | Logic / matrix puzzles | ⚠️ partial (brainteasers exist untimed) | Add a **timed cognitive/logic mode** (grid/matching/matrix) mirroring PEAK6/CTC aptitude batteries — lower priority; can live as a "timed toggle" on Brainteasers rather than in the arena. |
| 8 | Combinatorics / counting | ❌ GAP (folds into #2) | Bundle into the **Timed Probability Sprint** as a counting sub-pack (Old Mission/XTX flavor). |
| 9 | Rules reading-comprehension | ❌ GAP (implicit) | Low priority as standalone; naturally covered by the **market-making game's** "read the rules, then act fast" framing. |
| 10 | Coding under time | Out of scope for trader track | Leave out of the arena; note per-firm that research/dev seats (XTX, Two Sigma, Virtu, Tower; and Citadel/DRW/HRT trader-adjacent) gate on it — belongs to the *Could*-priority coding track, not Speed Arena. |

### C.3 What prior planning already captured (extend, don't duplicate)
- `DESIGN_TIMING_LEADERBOARD.md` already specifies the **arithmetic gate, penalty scoring, rushing detection, EV coaching, leaderboard/leagues, and a soft "timed toggle" for reasoning tabs.** → The **Timed Probability Sprint / Sequences / Estimation** ideas above are best implemented as instances of that already-designed **"soft timed toggle"** (untimed by default, never blocks) rather than new penalty-scored leaderboard modes. **Do not re-litigate the leaderboard design — reuse it.**
- `FIRM_REQUIREMENTS.md §3` already flags **Estimation/Fermi, market-making games, and game-theory/poker** as course-coverage gaps and folds them into Interview Games / Calibration Gym. → The arena work should **connect to** those surfaces (e.g., timed estimation feeds Calibration Gym), not build parallel content.
- **New vs. prior:** the concrete **firm presets (Flow 60/6, Maven 50/5, Belvedere mixed), the no-skip/MCQ toggles, the dedicated timed Sequences and timed Arbitrage/de-vig drills, and the P&L-tracking market-making shot-clock** are **not** yet specified in either prior doc — these are the net-new, research-justified additions.
- **Quick win:** expand `firmFormats.ts` with Flow/Maven/Belvedere/CTC entries (dated, `confidence: medium`, community-reported caveat) so the arena's firm attribution reflects Part-2 firms, not just Optiver/Jane Street.
