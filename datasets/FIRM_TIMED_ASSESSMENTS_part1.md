# Firm Timed Assessments — Part 1 (10 firms)

**Scope:** Undergrad quant-**trader** internship online assessments (OAs) and timed interview elements at 10 top prop/market-making firms. Research to inform a *timed-drills* feature — no synthesis or feature recommendations here (handled by a parallel worker).

**Confidence tags:** `[OFFICIAL]` = firm/vendor source · `[COMMUNITY]` = candidate reports / prep-vendor aggregation (directionally accurate, specifics vary by cycle). Timings/thresholds fluctuate year-to-year; treat exact numbers as approximate unless marked official. Research date: **2026-07-28**.

## 1. Optiver  *(anchor — most detail)*

**Timed OA: YES.** Delivered as an **SHL-administered** battery (candidate-reported as **Optiver's own SHL suite**, sometimes proctored on a video call), typically an unsupervised window (~72 hrs) to complete all sections; **failing any single section ends the process**. Full battery reported at ~2h45m including a personality test. `[COMMUNITY]`

**Sub-assessments (candidate/vendor-reported names):**
- **80-in-8 ("Mental Math"):** ~80 arithmetic questions in **8 minutes** (~6 sec/question). Multiplication, division, fractions, decimals, percentages, **with negatives**. Multiple choice (4 options). **Scoring: +1 correct, −1 incorrect, −1 skipped** (so blind guessing is EV≈0, skipping is −1 → guess rather than skip). No calculator; pen/paper allowed but rarely useful at 6 s/q. Community pass ≈ 55–56; competitive ≈ 70+. `[COMMUNITY]`
- **NumberLogic ("Sequences"):** number/pattern-completion — find the next term. `[COMMUNITY]`
- **Zap-N:** rapid numerical/estimation-style section. `[COMMUNITY]`
- **Beat the Odds ("Probability"):** probability under time — reportedly ~**15 min**, quick EV/probability-of-event questions. `[COMMUNITY]`
- Candidate (user) also reports: **arbitrage-finding** across several goods given bid/ask spreads, and **Fermi/estimation** (e.g., "how many squares in a box"). Consistent with Optiver's own practice tools (Number Box, Sequences, Probability, Technology tools). `[COMMUNITY]`

**Question categories:** fast mental math (incl. negatives/fractions), sequences/pattern recognition, probability/EV under time, estimation/Fermi, spread/arbitrage reasoning, adaptive difficulty (SHL sections scale up).

**Interview-round timed elements:** Numerical & probability round (~30–45 min, EV + brainteasers, conversational — reasoning valued); **market-making game** where interviewer sets an unknown quantity and you quote two-sided markets while they trade against you; rapid mental-math can appear "out of the blue."

**Sources:** [The Interview Den — Optiver](https://theinterviewden.com/companies/optiver-trading-interview) `[COMMUNITY]`; [TechInterview — Optiver guide](https://www.techinterview.org/companies/optiver-interview-guide/) `[COMMUNITY]`; [Quantt — Optiver](https://www.quantt.co.uk/resources/optiver-interview) `[COMMUNITY]`; [SpaceComplexity — Zetamac/Mental Math](https://spacecomplexity.ai/blog/zetamac-mental-math-quant-interview) `[COMMUNITY]`; [TestHQ — Optiver SHL](https://www.testhq.com/blog/optiver-shl-test) `[COMMUNITY, prep-vendor]`.

## 2. Jane Street

**Timed OA: MOSTLY interview-embedded, not a pure sprint.** Reports diverge: some describe a standalone **~7–8 min timed mental-math test ("Zetamac-style," ~60–90 Qs)** as a gate; others describe arithmetic **embedded into interview rounds** (3–5 Qs/round) where the interviewer **asks your confidence level after each answer** — so reasoning/calibration matters as much as speed. A HackerRank-style OA (~60–90 min: probability/logic, mental math, 2–3 Python problems) is also reported for some tracks. `[COMMUNITY]`

**Timing / thresholds:** mental-math ~7–8 min; soft pass ~35–40 correct, strong 50+, top profiles 70+. No calculator; scratch paper generally not used. `[COMMUNITY]`

**Question categories:** two-digit multiplication, percentages, fractions/decimals; probability puzzles & brainteasers (complement counting, conditional probability, EV); confidence estimation; light market-making; some coding.

**Interview-round timed elements:** multi-round virtual onsite — probability puzzles (2–3/round), **mental-math sprint round**, **market-making game**, behavioral. Intern pool skews Putnam/olympiad.

**Sources:** [The Interview Den — Jane Street](https://theinterviewden.com/companies/jane-street-quant-trader-interview) `[COMMUNITY]`; [TechInterview — Jane Street](https://www.techinterview.org/companies/jane-street/) `[COMMUNITY]`; [Myntbit — Jane Street](https://myntbit.com/blog/jane-street-quant-interview-guide-2026) `[COMMUNITY]`; [SpaceComplexity](https://spacecomplexity.ai/blog/zetamac-mental-math-quant-interview) `[COMMUNITY]`.

## 3. Citadel Securities (and Citadel)

**Timed OA: YES.** Cognitive-ability OA reported as **~50 questions in 12 minutes** mixing **logic, arithmetic, and English/verbal** (~14 s/q). A separate account describes **15 questions in 30 minutes** — likely a different track/cycle. Traders are usually sent **two OAs within ~1 week**. Intern OA ≈ full-time OA; ~70% do not pass. `[COMMUNITY]`

**Question categories:** rapid arithmetic, logic/pattern recognition, verbal reasoning, some probability. Mental math ≈ ~25% of the trader screen weight (per aggregator). Coding tracks may add HackerRank/Codility puzzles.

**Interview-round timed elements:** Super Day of 4–6 domain-specific interviews (probability, coding, market intuition, behavioral); **out-of-the-blue quick-maths** (e.g., "7×14?", "√225?") to test reaction under uncertainty; think-aloud is expected.

**Sources:** [EverythingQuant — Citadel trader](https://everythingquant.com/guides/quantitative-trader-at-citadel/) `[COMMUNITY]`; [TradingInterview — Citadel Securities OA](https://www.tradinginterview.com/courses/company-preparations-course/lessons/citadel-securities/topic/citadel-securities-online-assessment/) `[COMMUNITY, prep-vendor]`; [Quantt — Citadel internship](https://www.quantt.co.uk/resources/citadel-internship) `[COMMUNITY]`; [SpaceComplexity](https://spacecomplexity.ai/blog/zetamac-mental-math-quant-interview) `[COMMUNITY]`.

## 4. Jump Trading

**Timed OA: YES (for trader/research).** ~**60–90 min** test: **rapid-fire mental arithmetic (30–60 problems)**, probability brainteasers, and (for dev-heavy tracks) an algorithmic coding section. SWE roles get a **90–120 min** coding OA (2–3 hard problems). Trader OA ≈ probability-and-statistics test of similar length. ~20–30% clearance (unofficial). `[COMMUNITY]`

**Question categories:** exact mental arithmetic under heavy speed emphasis (Jump prizes raw computational speed), probability/EV, brainteasers, coding (C++/Python) for relevant tracks.

**Interview-round timed elements:** phone screens with 2–3 probability Qs + a mental-arithmetic check + a coding Q; Super Day of 5–7 back-to-back 45–60 min rounds including a **mental-math speed round**, probability deep-dive, C++, and market-intuition (specific historical market events).

**Sources:** [Myntbit — Jump](https://myntbit.com/blog/jump-trading-quant-interview-guide) `[COMMUNITY]`; [Quantt — Jump](https://www.quantt.co.uk/resources/jump-trading-interview) `[COMMUNITY]`.

## 5. IMC Trading

**Timed OA: YES — the decisive filter.** ~**60–75 min** timed test; for traders the focus is **mental arithmetic (~50–80 questions)** — two-digit multiplication, percentages, fractions, division — **plus a sequence/pattern-logic section**. Scored on **accuracy under time (no partial credit)**. SWE candidates instead get 2 HackerRank problems in ~90 min. `[COMMUNITY]`

**Question categories:** two-digit multiplication (target <3 s/q is an edge), percentages/fractions/decimals, addition/subtraction/division interleaved to break rhythm, numerical sequences/pattern recognition. Reported day-of split ≈ 35% mental math, 30% probability/EV, 25% market intuition/games, 10% behavioral.

**Interview-round timed elements:** OA → phone screen → Super Day (4–5 interviews + trading game); **rapid-fire arithmetic throughout the day** ("under five seconds" per interviewer prompt); make-a-market games. OA reputationally meritocratic (high scorers advance regardless of school — varies by cycle).

**Sources:** [Quantt — IMC](https://www.quantt.co.uk/resources/imc-trading-interview) `[COMMUNITY]`; [The Interview Den — IMC](https://theinterviewden.com/companies/imc-trading-interview) `[COMMUNITY]`; [SpaceComplexity](https://spacecomplexity.ai/blog/zetamac-mental-math-quant-interview) `[COMMUNITY]`.

## 6. SIG (Susquehanna)

**Timed OA: YES — via Mercer | Mettl.** Two reported formats (role/cycle dependent):
- **Quantitative Evaluation** (trader track): **~12–16 questions in 20 minutes** (puzzle-style math + logic; linear systems, EV, grid path-finding). Older format cited as 14–16 Qs / 20 min; **now partly supplanted**. Hard per-question time pressure; some sections 15–30 s/q, no calculator. `[COMMUNITY / prep-vendor]`
- **Problem-Solving Assessment** (research/newer): **~9–17 open-ended questions in ~60 min**, **calculator allowed** — tests thinking style; mainly EV/probability with 1–2 deductive-reasoning or single-variable-calculus items. `[COMMUNITY / prep-vendor]`
- One aggregator describes a combined **60–75 min, ~30–50 Qs** across mental arithmetic, probability/EV, sequences, short estimation. Only **one attempt per cycle**. `[COMMUNITY]`

**Question categories:** probability & conditional probability (Bayes), EV, quick/large-number arithmetic, logic propositions, optimization, sequences/estimation.

**Interview-round timed elements:** phone/video probability rounds (~60 min, reasoning-focused); Super Day 4–6 back-to-back; the signature **Poker Round** (EV, pot odds, Bayesian updating, bet-sizing under social pressure — tutorial provided, no prior expertise assumed).

**Sources:** [Tradermath — SIG](https://www.tradermath.org/articles/sig-interview-guide) `[COMMUNITY, prep-vendor]`; [JobTestPrep — SIG OA](https://www.jobtestprep.com/sig-online-assessment) `[COMMUNITY, prep-vendor]`; [EverythingQuant — SIG OA cycles](https://everythingquant.com/forum/post/sig-quant-trading-internship-oa-cycles-guide/) `[COMMUNITY]`; [Quantt — SIG](https://www.quantt.co.uk/resources/sig-interview) `[COMMUNITY]`.

## 7. DRW

**Timed OA: YES (for traders/quant).** Trader OA ~**60–75 min** mixing **mental math, probability, and short market-knowledge** questions. Intern quant/QR OA reported as **6–8 questions in ~45–60 min** (mathematical reasoning, probability & stats, brainteasers) — one 2026 report: **6 questions in 45 min (~7.5 min/q)**. SWE track: 2 HackerRank problems in ~90 min. Bar high but not extreme (~25–30% advance). `[COMMUNITY]`

**Question categories:** expectation/EV, conditional probability, recursion (e.g., collect-all-N-sides, absorbing states), DP/state modeling, linear algebra & calculus basics, sequences, mental math. Emphasis on **compute correctly under severe time pressure** (numpy/scipy useful; reusable templates save time on the QR track).

**Interview-round timed elements:** 1–2 phone/video rounds (brainteasers + market-intuition; engineers: live coding); Super Day of 4–6× 45-min sessions, desk-specific content (rates, options Greeks/vol, crypto microstructure). Cross-desk avg ≈ 30% probability, 25% mental math, 25% market intuition, 20% behavioral.

**Sources:** [Quantt — DRW](https://www.quantt.co.uk/resources/drw-interview) `[COMMUNITY]`; [dev.to — DRW 2026 QR OA review](https://dev.to/net_programhelp_e160eef28/drw-2026-summer-quantqr-intern-oa-full-review-3cj2) `[COMMUNITY]`; [EverythingQuant — DRW OA](https://everythingquant.com/forum/post/drw-trading-internship-oa--difficulty--timelines/) `[COMMUNITY]`.

## 8. Hudson River Trading (HRT)

**Timed OA: YES for algo/quant-trader/research (multi-stage).** Reported 3-stage structure inside a tight window:
1. **CodeSignal General Coding Assessment** (bar ~78; <70 auto-decline).
2. **Math/probability stage** — ships to Algo/Quant Trader/Research only: **~8–12 questions in 60 min (~5 min/q), multiple-choice, pick-the-answer (no derivations)**; Quant Trader variant reported at 90 min. **One pass; timer runs on network drops.**
3. Systems-implementation stage for infra/perf tracks. `[COMMUNITY]`

Also reported (algo-trading intern): timed **mental math + probability puzzles + logic**, where the **speed requirement** is the real difficulty even when questions look straightforward. `[COMMUNITY]`

**Question categories:** coding (CodeSignal), probability/EV (expected flips to N consecutive heads, expectation of max of 3 U[0,1] draws, Monty Hall variants), estimation, timed mental math/logic.

**Interview-round timed elements:** virtual onsite ~5 rounds (coding + math/probability + estimation chats). Note: HRT's *engineering* track has no meaningful arithmetic-sprint gate per aggregators — the math sprint is the trader/algo path.

**Sources:** [OA VO Service — HRT 3-stage](https://oavoservice.com/en/articles/hrt-oa-comprehensive-three-stage-guide) `[COMMUNITY]`; [EverythingQuant — HRT OA](https://everythingquant.com/forum/post/hudson-river-trading-algo-trading-intern-oa--full-guide/) `[COMMUNITY]`; [SpaceComplexity](https://spacecomplexity.ai/blog/zetamac-mental-math-quant-interview) `[COMMUNITY]`.

## 9. Five Rings

**Timed OA: YES — reputed hardest math sprint.** **HackerRank-hosted, proctored** "Quant Trader Screen": **~15–20 open-ended questions in ~20 minutes** (~1 min/q). **Typed numerical answers** (accuracy to ~2nd decimal or nearest integer), **no multiple choice, no calculator**, one question at a time with a visible countdown. Questions are **designed so they can't be solved exactly** — reward finding a simplified model/shortcut fast. `[COMMUNITY, prep-vendor]`

**Question categories (two broad buckets):** (1) **Probability & combinatorics** (coin/dice models, multi-step puzzles, Markov chains, EV with careful conditioning); (2) **Estimation & mental math** (Fermi-style, olympiad-flavored, quick approximation).

**Interview-round timed elements:** phone screens on probability/combinatorics/math reasoning (process + communication graded); reported onsite began with **fast-paced estimation-style questions**, then math (probability, stochastic processes) + game theory. Some candidates get an offer without a formal superday.

**Sources:** [Aptitude Test Prep — Five Rings OA](https://aptitude-test-prep.com/employers/trading-assessments/five-rings-online-assessment/) `[COMMUNITY, prep-vendor]`; [Wall Street Oasis — Five Rings intern interview](https://www.wallstreetoasis.com/company/five-rings-capital-llc/interview/quant-trading-intern-0) `[COMMUNITY]`; [Quant Blueprint — Five Rings](https://www.quantblueprint.com/guides/how-to-get-a-job-at-five-rings) `[COMMUNITY]`.

## 10. Akuna Capital

**Timed OA: YES — HackerRank, two sections then a coding round.** First OA: `[COMMUNITY, prep-vendor]`
- **Calculations (Mental Math):** **80 questions in 8 minutes** ("80-in-8"), **free response (no multiple choice, type each answer)**, integers & decimals, **no fractions**; **no calculator, pen/paper discouraged**. Scoring reported +1/−1 (target ~56). ~32 s/q effective per one report, but pure speed is the point.
- **Sequences:** **~24–30 questions in 12–16 minutes** — find the next element; not only numeric (letters/logical patterns too).
- Must pass **both** to reach the **second OA: HackerRank coding test** (2–3 questions, easy–medium; e.g., a P&L/"Playing the Markets" simulation) + a **VidCruiter video interview with a betting/strategy game**.

> Note: Because Akuna's mental-math is free-response with no fractions, Zetamac-style integer drilling transfers cleanly (unlike Optiver/SIG which add fractions).

**Question categories:** raw mental arithmetic (multiplication/division-heavy), sequence/pattern recognition, coding, strategic betting/EV game.

**Interview-round timed elements:** technical interview with **brainteasers + market-making games + probability + basic derivatives**; final round technical + HR.

**Sources:** [Aptitude Test Prep — Akuna math test](https://aptitude-test-prep.com/employers/trading-assessments/akuna-capital-math-test/) `[COMMUNITY, prep-vendor]`; [TradingInterview — Akuna OA](https://www.tradinginterview.com/akuna-capital-online-assessment/) `[COMMUNITY, prep-vendor]`; [dev.to — Akuna intern OA](https://dev.to/net_programhelp_e160eef28/akuna-capital-trading-intern-oa-full-experience-process-breakdown-501f) `[COMMUNITY]`; [Tradermath — Akuna](https://www.tradermath.org/knowledge-base/akuna-capital-interview-guide) `[COMMUNITY, prep-vendor]`; [SpaceComplexity](https://spacecomplexity.ai/blog/zetamac-mental-math-quant-interview) `[COMMUNITY]`.

### Deliverable summary

The standout timed formats across these 10 firms cluster into three archetypes: (1) the **pure arithmetic sprint** — Optiver's and Akuna's **"80-in-8"** (~6 s/question), with Optiver adding fractions/negatives and +1/−1 penalty scoring while Akuna is free-response with no fractions; (2) the **short high-difficulty problem set** — Five Rings' ~15–20 typed questions in ~20 min (reputedly the hardest) and DRW's ~6–8 questions in ~45–60 min (~7.5 min/q, recursion/EV-heavy), plus HRT's ~8–12 multiple-choice math questions in 60 min; and (3) the **mixed cognitive/probability battery** — IMC and Jump (arithmetic + probability, 60–90 min), Citadel Securities (~50 logic/arithmetic/verbal Qs in 12 min), and SIG's Mercer|Mettl quant eval (~20 min) or 60-min problem-solving set. Jane Street is the notable outlier, embedding arithmetic into interview rounds and grading **answer + stated confidence** rather than raw sprint speed. Nearly every firm also carries timed elements into interviews (rapid-fire mental math, market-making games, and — uniquely — SIG's poker round). All specifics are community/prep-vendor reported (dated 2026-07-28) and vary by cycle; treat exact counts and thresholds as approximate.
